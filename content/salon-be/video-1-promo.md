### Behooz's line

He should say this in his own words on the day. Prompt him with *"What do people come to you for?"* and use the best take. Starting points if he wants one:

- "I'm Behooz. Colour is my thing. Blondes especially."
- "I'm Behooz. I've been cutting hair for twenty years, and I still love a good blow wave."
- "I'm Behooz. If you want it done right the first time, come see me."
- "I'm Behooz. I'll tell you what your hair actually needs. Then I'll do it."

Keep it to one breath. If a take runs long, cut it in the edit rather than reshooting.

### Captions and posting

**Burnt-in captions:** Yes, every spoken line. Most people watch muted.

**Caption copy (post text):**
> Six years in Merivale. Thirty years' experience in every chair.
> Colour, cuts, keratin, blow waves. We talk before we touch.
> 📍 54 Holmwood Road · Book at salonbe.co.nz

**Hashtags (keep to 5):**
`#salonbe #merivale #christchurchhair #chchsalon #hairchristchurch`

**Link:** Use the clean URL `salonbe.co.nz` everywhere on screen and in captions. Drop the ad-tracking parameters from the Google Ads link; they add nothing for organic and look wrong on a title card.
