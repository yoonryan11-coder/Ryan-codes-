Yoonspace started in 2024 with one belief: most brands don't need reinventing — they need a voice.

We're a digital marketing studio in Ōtautahi Christchurch, working with businesses across Aotearoa — hospitality, entertainment and service brands ready to be seen. We don't chase trends or vanity metrics. We find what's already true about you, say it in a way people actually feel, and put real craft and budget behind it.

That's the difference between a post that disappears overnight and marketing that compounds. We build community first, because community is what keeps people coming back long after a one-off campaign would have faded.

From a fried chicken shop that hit its first million in a year, to a salon booked out on an $80 ad spend, the throughline is always the same: voice the brand, build the community, grow the numbers.
