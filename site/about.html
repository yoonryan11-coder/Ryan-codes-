About

A studio from a city that started again.

Ōtautahi
Christchurch
since 2014

When we started, half the central city was still hoardings and gravel.

Christchurch was rebuilding — not just patching things up, but deciding what it wanted to be next. That's the spirit Yoonspace grew out of: the belief that the best time to make a bold move is when everyone's watching to see who steps up.

We're a full-service studio, which means we don't hand you between five agencies who don't talk to each other. The same people who shape your strategy build your website, run your ads and answer the phone. Less lost in translation, more momentum.

We work with ambitious New Zealand businesses — the roaster going national, the trade firm done blending in, the founder who knows the product is good and just needs the world to notice. We clear the space, and we help you grow into it.
