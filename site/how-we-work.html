How we work

From first call to full orbit.

The actual sequence, start to finish. Four of the steps are things you book yourself — the rest is us doing the work.

Every campaign is built on three pillars

  • Brand awareness
  • Product
  • Community

Blocks run in 4-week cycles. Steps 05 – 07 repeat, block after block — that's the part that compounds.

  1. 01
    15 min · Free

    Quick consultation

    A free call about your business and what you want it to do. We listen, ask the right questions, and tell you honestly whether we can help.

    Book a consultation →
  2. 02
    Strategy

    Create your content pillars

    We set your three pillars — brand awareness, product and community — and lock in a content plan that works with how your business actually runs.

  3. 03
    1 or 3 months · Quoted

    Choose your retainer

    A 1-month retainer for a short-term push, or 3 months for long-term growth. Your goals decide which — we'll recommend, you choose. Pricing is quoted to your brief.

    Start a project →
  4. 04
    Once confirmed

    Get set up

    You get the onboarding form and we lock in your storyboard session dates — and filming sessions too, where we can. No passwords, just proper access.

    Already confirmed? Fill in the onboarding form →
  5. 05
    Storyboard session

    Plan it in detail

    We confirm the filming session and get into the finer detail of every idea. The campaign brief comes out of this — it's what we script and plan from.

    Book a storyboard session →
  6. 06
    Filming shoot

    Film, edit, launch

    We film to the brief, edit it, and your campaigns start within 3 – 7 working days of the shoot.

    Lock in a filming day →
  7. 07
    4-week blocks

    Run the block, then go again

    Each block is four weeks. The goal is to maximise content across all three pillars, read the numbers, and plan the next one. General meetings keep it on track.

    Book a general meeting →

Ready when you are
